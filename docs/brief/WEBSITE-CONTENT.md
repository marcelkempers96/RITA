## 13 Profiles
**Gideon Ndubuisi** · Programme lead · Delft
Assistant Professor of Economics at Delft University of Technology (TU Delft). His research covers the political economy of Africa's green structural transformation, with a focus on green industrialisation, transition minerals and industrial policy, alongside trade, global value chains and productivity. He writes RITA's weekly *Policy Insights*. He has worked with UNIDO, the World Bank, the African Development Bank, the European Commission, ACET, UNU-WIDER, APRI and the IFC, and was previously at the German Development Institute (IDOS), where he helped set up the Research Network Sustainable Supply Chains. PhD, UNU-MERIT / Maastricht University. **[confirm]**
Tags: Green industrialisation · Transition minerals · Industrial policy · Global value chains · Productivity
Links: Email · LinkedIn · Google Scholar · https://sites.google.com/view/gideonndubuisi

**Elvis Korku Avenyo** · Programme lead · Johannesburg
Associate Professor of Development Economics at the SARChI Chair in Industrial Development, University of Johannesburg. His work spans innovation, technology adoption, industrial development, labour markets and firm behaviour, including the informal economy. He was previously a Senior Researcher at CCRED and a Research Officer at Oxford's Technology and Management Centre for Development (TMCD). PhD, UNU-MERIT / Maastricht University. Awards: 2025 Middleton Award; Best Conference Paper, African Economic Conference 2022.
Tags: Innovation · Industrial development · Digital technologies · Labour markets · Informality
Links: Email · LinkedIn · Google Scholar · https://sites.google.com/view/elvis-korku-avenyo

## 17 About `/about`
- **H1:** Africa-led research on how the continent can industrialise through the green transition
- **Intro:** RITA brings rigorous economics to the decisions shaping Africa's minerals, energy and industry.
- **Approach:** *Research.* Firm-level evidence and rigorous methods. · *Policy.* Weekly insights and commissioned advice. · *Transformation.* Jobs, capabilities and structural change.
- **Our story:** RITA grew out of a long collaboration between Gideon Ndubuisi and Elvis Korku Avenyo, who met at UNU-MERIT and have worked together since 2018: on robotisation and exports, value chains and innovation, South Africa's electricity crises, gender and the green transition, and a 2025 World Bank report on regional mineral value chains. RITA makes that collaboration an institution that is Africa-led, open to fellows from across the continent, and focused on evidence that policymakers can use. **[founders to edit]**
- **Mission:** To produce rigorous, independent evidence on how African economies can industrialise through the green transition, and to put it in the hands of those who decide.
- **Values:** Evidence first · Africa-led · Independent · Open (publish openly wherever rights allow) · Useful.

- **Kicker:** Contribute · **H1:** Publish with RITA
- **Intro:** RITA researchers, fellows and invited contributors can submit Policy Insights, blogs, op-eds and papers for editing and publication.
- **Buttons:** Start a submission · Read guidelines · My submissions
- **Formats:** Policy Insight, one idea in 600–900 words (weekly series) · Blog / op-ed, 800–1,200 words (commentary; we can also help place it in the press) · Policy brief, 4 pages (recommendations for decision-makers) · Working paper, PDF upload (RITA Working Paper Series)
- **How it works:** 01 Submit (write in the editor or upload a Word file) · 02 Editorial review (response within 5 working days) · 03 Edits (comments and revisions in your dashboard) · 04 Publish (scheduled, then shared in the newsletter and on social)
- **Guidelines:** Plain English, evidence-led, no jargon. One clear argument per piece. Cite sources with links. Lead with the policy point. Images: only use images you own or have permission to use, with a credit and alt text for each.
- **Questions:** editor@rita.africa
